Frequently Asked Questions

Which areas of Lagos do you cover?

We currently serve Ikeja, Maryland, Gbagada, Yaba, Surulere, Victoria Island, Ikoyi, Lekki Phase 1, Ajah, Festac, Ogba, Magodo and Ikorodu. A small travel fee may apply depending on your area — it's always shown before you pay.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies or equipment?

No. Our cleaners arrive with all professional supplies and equipment. If you prefer we use your own products (e.g. for allergies), just mention it in the booking notes.

How is the price calculated?

Price = service base price + amount per bedroom/bathroom + any add-ons + travel fee − recurring-plan discount. Use the Price Calculator to see your exact price before booking — there are no hidden charges.

How do I pay?

You can pay securely online via Paystack (card, bank transfer or USSD) when booking, or choose "pay after service" and pay cash/transfer once the job is done.

Can I reschedule or cancel a booking?

Yes — free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment, from your dashboard or the booking tracking page. Cancellations under 24 hours may attract a 20% fee.

Are your cleaners vetted?

Every cleaner is identity-verified, background-checked, trained and works in uniform. You can see your assigned cleaner's name on the tracking page before they arrive.

What if I'm not happy with the cleaning?

We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. Report any issue within 24 hours and we'll re-clean the affected areas free of charge.

Do you offer recurring plans?

Yes! Weekly (save 15%), bi-weekly (save 10%) and monthly (save 5%) plans. Pick your plan during booking and we'll keep the same cleaner where possible.

How do before/after photos work?

Your cleaner uploads photos before starting and after finishing. They appear on your booking tracking page so you can verify the result — even if you're not home.

Can I book for someone else (e.g. my parents)?

Absolutely. Just enter their address and phone number during booking, and keep your own email for updates and the receipt.

Still have questions?

We reply within minutes on WhatsApp during working hours.
