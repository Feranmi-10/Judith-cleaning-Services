Privacy Policy

What we collect

Name, phone number, email, service address and booking details — only what we need to deliver your cleaning and keep you updated.

How we use it

To process bookings and payments, assign cleaners, send booking updates by email/WhatsApp/SMS, and improve our services. We never sell your data.

Payments

Card details are processed directly by Paystack over an encrypted connection. We never see or store your card numbers.

Photos

Before/after photos are taken only of the areas cleaned, used for quality verification on your booking page, and published publicly only with your consent.

Your rights

You may request a copy or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the email address on our Contact page.
